# Weapon Price from Recycle Value

[[Brotato]] › [[Tips]]

> [!tip]
> A weapon's current shop price is about **4 × its recycle value**. Look at the recycle value of a copy you own (same weapon, same tier) to know what it costs in the shop right now.

## Why
- Recycling pays `25%` of the weapon's current price, rounded down ([[Prices and Rerolls#Recycling]]). So the shop price is `4 × recycle value`, or up to `3` more.
- Both values grow with the wave and your items price %, so the rule holds all run.
- Use it before rerolling: you know whether you can afford the weapon if the reroll offers it.

## Good with
- Weapons you already own
- Items, if a mod that lets you recycle items is installed and working

## Bad with
- Recycling gains % (e.g. from [[Entrepreneur]]): recycling then pays `25% + gains`, so divide by that instead of multiplying by 4.
- Endless mode: past the last wave, the recycle value ignores your items price %, so the ×4 no longer matches.
