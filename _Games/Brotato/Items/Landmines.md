# Landmines

## Effects
- **Landmines**
  - spawns every 12s
  - `10` damage (100% Engineering)
  - Explodes on hit (100% chance, explosion size 175%)

> [!note] Structure
> Uses structure % damage, structure range and **structure attack speed** (e.g. [[Improved Tools]]), not your attack speed. See [[Structures and Pets]].

## How it works
- Places a landmine every `12s`, and one at the start of each wave. Structure attack speed shortens the timer (whole seconds).
- A mine explodes **once** when an enemy steps on it: 10 damage (100% Engineering), explosion size 175%.

## Cursed
Worst roll `+10%` (wave 1) → best roll `+110%` (wave 21+). Each effect rolls on its own. How it works: [[Curse#Curse strength]].
