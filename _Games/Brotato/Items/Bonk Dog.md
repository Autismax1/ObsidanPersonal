# Bonk Dog

[[Brotato]] › [[Items]] › Tier 2

## Effects
- Pet bonk dog (10 damage (60% Melee damage), cooldown 0.47s, range 150, explosion: 8 damage (40% Melee damage), cooldown 5.00s, range 150, 100% chance, explosion size 150%)

## How it works
- Bites nearby enemies: 10 damage (60% Melee damage), attack cooldown 0.47s.
- Every 5s it leaps onto the enemy with the **most max HP** (not one right next to it) and explodes on landing: 8 damage (40% Melee damage), explosion size 150%.
- No valid target: it retries after 2s. The first leap of a wave comes after a random 1.25–5s.
- Pets get your % Damage and Crit chance, but **not your Attack speed**: their attack cooldowns never change.
