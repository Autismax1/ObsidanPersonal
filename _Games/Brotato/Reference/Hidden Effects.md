# Hidden Effects

[[Brotato]] › Hidden Effects

Effects marked `[EMPTY]` in the game data. They work, but the tooltip doesn't show them.

## Characters
| Character | Hidden effect |
|---|---|
| [[Mad Scientist]] | **+100% mutation fruit chance**. See [[Evolution Drops#Mutation fruit]]. |
| [[Generalist]] | max 3 ranged weapons |
| [[Explorer]] | trees from wave 1 |
| [[Beast Master]] | boosted wanted tags: 10% instead of 5%. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Wanted tags]]. |
| [[Builder]] | max 1 turret |
| [[Gangster]] | lock flag |

## Items
- All 8 **fossils** have a hidden "counts as fossil" effect, used by fossil synergies.
- **Mutation** can copy that fossil count into the Tumor. See [[Mutation]].

## Danger 6 (Nightmare)
The tooltip folds elites and double boss into "everything from Danger 5". The one effect it never mentions:
- **+10% inflation**: item prices grow a bit faster per wave. See [[Prices and Rerolls]] and [[Danger Levels#Danger 6 (Nightmare)]].
