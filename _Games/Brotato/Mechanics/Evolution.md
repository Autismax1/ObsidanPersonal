# Evolution

[[Brotato]] › Primal Dread · flowchart: [[Evolution Flow.canvas|Evolution Flow]]

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Evolution **> 0 = meca**, **< 0 = mutant**.
> - Past `±2` you're locked to a side: only your fruit drops and your side's items join the shop.
> - Meca fruit `+1`, mutant fruit `−1` ([[Evolution Drops#Meca and mutant fruit]]), every mutation `−1` on the Tumor ([[Mutation#Step 4: the Tumor grows]]), first mechanize of a weapon `+1` ([[Meca]]).

^glance

## How evolution changes
- Characters like [[Droid]] (+1 per wave) or [[Mad Scientist]] (+2 per wave) gain it on their own.
- Primal Dread items carry evolution effects.

## The ±2 threshold
- **Shop pool:**
  - Above +2: meca items join the pool.
  - Below −2: mutant items join the pool.
  - Either way: the shared evolution items join too.
  - Dual evolution above +2 also adds the mutant items.
- Between −2 and +2 both fruits drop 50/50 and no side items appear.

## What each side does
| | Meca (evolution > 0) | Mutant (evolution < 0) |
|---|---|---|
| Upgrades | spend parts to [[Meca\|mechanize weapons]] | [[Mutation\|mutate items]] at wave end |
| Currency | [[Evolution Drops#Meca parts\|meca parts]] | [[Evolution Drops#Mutation fruit\|mutation fruit]] |
| Strength from | weapon rank | how negative your evolution is |
| Enemies | [[Evolved Enemies\|meca enemies]] | [[Evolved Enemies\|mutant enemies]] |

## Special characters
- **Meca only** ([[Droid]]): mutation fruit does nothing.
- **Dual evolution** ([[Mad Scientist]]): fruit is 50/50, mutation works at any non-zero evolution, and enemies evolve to both sides.
