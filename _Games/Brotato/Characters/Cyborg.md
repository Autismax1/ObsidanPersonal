# Cyborg

[[Brotato]] › [[Characters]] · during a run: [[Run Cheat Sheet]]

## Starting effects
- +200% gains of Ranged damage
- Convert temp half wave
- -75% gains of Engineering
- -100% gains of Melee damage
- -100% gains of Elemental damage

**Starting weapon choices:** Wrench, Screwdriver, Plank, Pistol, Smg, Revolver, Double barrel shotgun, Crossbow, Brick, Awl, Crowbar, Machinegun, Carbon bow, Sticky grenade

## Shop
- **Wanted tags:** [[Wanted Tags#Structure|Structure]], [[Wanted Tags#Ranged damage|Ranged damage]]
  On 5% of item rolls the shop only offers items with one of these tags. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Wanted tags]].
- **Banned item groups:** [[Item Groups#Melee damage|Melee damage]] (Mastery, Goat skull, Riposte); [[Item Groups#Elemental damage|Elemental damage]] (Strange book, Frozen heart, Boiling water, Snowball, Toxic sludge, Fuel tank, Will o the wisp)
- **Banned items (2):** Charcoal, Gnome
- Bans stop after the last wave: in endless these items can appear. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Character bans]].

## Level-ups
- **Never offered:** Melee damage, Elemental damage
- Level-up rarity follows your level, see [[Rarity Odds#Level-ups]].

## Evolution and curse
- **[[Meca]] never offers:** Melee damage, Elemental damage. See [[Meca#The four stat groups]].
- **[[Mutation]] can copy these character effects into the Tumor** (scaled down, see [[Mutation#Copy strength]]):
  - +200% gains of Ranged damage
  - Not if that stat is already at its cap.
- Curse chances: [[Curse]] · enemy variants: [[Evolved Enemies]], [[Cursed Enemies]]
