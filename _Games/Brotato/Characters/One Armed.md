# One Armed

[[Brotato]] › [[Characters]] · during a run: [[Run Cheat Sheet]]

## Starting effects
- Attack speed +200
- +100% gains of Percent damage, Ranged damage, Melee damage, Elemental damage, Explosion damage, Piercing damage, Bounce damage, Damage against bosses
- One slot limit (-5)
- Set bonus (+3)

**Starting weapon choices:** Claw, Dagger, Ghost axe, Lightning shiv, Scissors, Cacti club, Torch, Plank, Hatchet, Pruner, Jousting lance, Double barrel shotgun, Shredder, Smg, Taser, Revolver, Icicle, Shuriken, Javelin, Crowbar, Shovel, Meat tenderizer, Whip, Blowgun, Sticky grenade, Molotov, Pan flute, Flail, Carbon bow, Slingshot

## Shop
- **Wanted tags:** [[Wanted Tags#Percent damage|Percent damage]]
  On 5% of item rolls the shop only offers items with one of these tags. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Wanted tags]].

- No banned level-ups.

## Evolution and curse
- [[Meca]] can offer every stat. See [[Meca#The four stat groups]].
- **[[Mutation]] can copy these character effects into the Tumor** (scaled down, see [[Mutation#Copy strength]]):
  - Attack speed +200
  - +100% gains of Percent damage, Ranged damage, Melee damage, Elemental damage, Explosion damage, Piercing damage, Bounce damage, Damage against bosses
  - Not if that stat is already at its cap.
- Curse chances: [[Curse]] · enemy variants: [[Evolved Enemies]], [[Cursed Enemies]]
