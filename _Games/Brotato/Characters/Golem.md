# Golem

[[Brotato]] › [[Characters]] · during a run: [[Run Cheat Sheet]]

## Starting effects
- Max hp +20
- +33% gains of Max hp
- +33% gains of Armor
- Below half health (+40)
- Below half health (+20)
- No heal

**Starting weapon choices:** Cacti club, Chopper, Fist, Ghost axe, Hatchet, Knife, Plank, Rock, Spiky shield, Stick, Torch, Pistol, Revolver, Slingshot, Crossbow, Ghost scepter, Brick, Spoon, Flute, Awl, Crowbar, Shovel, Whip, Molotov

## Shop
- **Wanted tags:** [[Wanted Tags#Max hp|Max hp]], [[Wanted Tags#Armor|Armor]]
  On 5% of item rolls the shop only offers items with one of these tags. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Wanted tags]].
- **Banned item groups:** [[Item Groups#Hp regeneration|Hp regeneration]] (Mushroom, Plant, Sad tomato, Medikit, Fairy, Potion, Fried rice, Baby squid, Coral, Penguin); [[Item Groups#Lifesteal|Lifesteal]] (Butterfly, Bat, Whetstone, Decomposing flesh, Bloody hand, Fresh meat); [[Item Groups#Lifesteal and hp regeneration|Lifesteal and hp regeneration]] (Blood leech); [[Item Groups#Consumable heal|Consumable heal]] (Jerky, Weird food, Lemonade, Fruit basket)
- **Banned items (9):** Tentacle, Goblet, Cute monkey, Turret healing, Garden, Extra stomach, Adrenaline, Weird ghost, Doc moth
- Bans stop after the last wave: in endless these items can appear. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Character bans]].

## Level-ups
- **Never offered:** Hp regeneration, Lifesteal
- Level-up rarity follows your level, see [[Rarity Odds#Level-ups]].

## Evolution and curse
- **[[Meca]] never offers:** Hp regeneration, Lifesteal. See [[Meca#The four stat groups]].
- **[[Mutation]] can copy these character effects into the Tumor** (scaled down, see [[Mutation#Copy strength]]):
  - Max hp +20
  - +33% gains of Max hp
  - +33% gains of Armor
  - Below half health (+40)
  - Below half health (+20)
  - Not if that stat is already at its cap.
- Curse chances: [[Curse]] · enemy variants: [[Evolved Enemies]], [[Cursed Enemies]]
