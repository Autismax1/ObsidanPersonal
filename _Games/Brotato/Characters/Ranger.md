# Ranger

[[Brotato]] › [[Characters]] · during a run: [[Run Cheat Sheet]]

## Starting effects
- Range +50
- +50% gains of Ranged damage
- No melee weapons
- -25% gains of Max hp

**Starting weapon choices:** [[Pistol]], [[Smg]], [[Revolver]], [[Double Barrel Shotgun]], [[Laser Gun]], [[Shredder]], [[Crossbow]], [[Slingshot]], [[Rail Gun]], [[Blowgun]], [[Sticky Grenade]], [[Machinegun]], [[Carbon Bow]]

## Shop
- **Wanted tags:** [[Wanted Tags#Ranged damage|Ranged damage]], [[Wanted Tags#Range|Range]]
  On 5% of item rolls the shop only offers items with one of these tags. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Wanted tags]].
- **Banned item groups:** [[Item Groups#Melee damage|Melee damage]] ([[Mastery]], [[Goat Skull]], [[Riposte]])
- Bans stop after the last wave: in endless these items can appear. See [[Shop Item Rolls#Character bans]].
